Data & Communication

Privacy Notice

Cool Base Hardware respects your privacy. We collect only information that you voluntarily choose to submit (such as your name, contact phone number, materials list, and delivery location) solely for preparing requested price estimates, completing deliveries, and processing orders.

We do not sell, rent, or distribute personal customer details to any third-party marketing services. When you engage with us via WhatsApp, messages are transferred via WhatsApp's end-to-end encrypted protocol and governed by WhatsApp's standard terms and privacy policies.

Your browser quote list is stored locally on your device via standard browser storage (`localStorage`) and is not transmitted to external servers unless you actively click to send your quotation over WhatsApp.

Guidelines

Terms of Service

Material descriptions and specifications displayed on this website are provided for project planning and estimation purposes. Because manufacturer costs and fuel charges vary periodically, all formal pricing, delivery fees, and order timelines must be confirmed directly through written quotation via WhatsApp or at our physical store before payment.

Calculators on this website provide general baseline estimates based on standard trade conventions (such as 1:2:4 concrete and 10 blocks/m²). Actual quantities required on site should always be verified by your project fundi, engineer, or contractor.

Materials dispatched for delivery remain the property of Cool Base Hardware until payment terms are fully satisfied upon arrival at the destination.
